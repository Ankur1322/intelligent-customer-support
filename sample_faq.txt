1. GENERAL BUSINESS WORKING HOURS
Our corporate offices are open Monday through Friday, from 8:00 AM to 6:00 PM Eastern Standard Time (EST). We are closed on all major US federal holidays. 

2. CONTACTING TECHNICAL SUPPORT
For direct technical support, customers may contact our help desk via email at support@supportai-corp.com or call our toll-free hotline at 1-800-555-0199 during corporate working hours. For emergency tier-3 issues outside of working hours, authorized admins can trigger a page on Slack via the channel #systems-emergency.

3. PASSWORD RESET PROTOCOLS
To securely reset your user password:
- Go to the login portal and click "Forgot Password".
- Enter your registered corporate email address.
- A secure 6-digit verification code will be sent to your email (expires in 10 minutes).
- Enter the 6-digit code on the screen and define your new secure password (must be at least 8 characters, containing 1 number and 1 special symbol).

4. REMOTE COMPILING NODES
We maintain compiling servers in US-East (Virginia) and EU-West (Ireland) regions. These compiling nodes automatically sync code structures every 15 minutes.
